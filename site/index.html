Our process

A clear process.
No unnecessary complexity.

Every project is different, but a clear process helps everyone understand what happens next. We keep communication direct and organize the work into practical stages.

01

Discover

We learn about your business, audience, goals, existing systems, and the problem you want to solve.

02

Define the scope

A written scope with deliverables, exclusions, milestones and a proposed timeline. Work begins once it's approved.

03

Plan & design

We organize the structure or product flows, set the visual direction, and prepare designs or prototypes for your review.

04

Develop

We build the agreed features, connect required services, and share progress at planned milestones.

05

Test & refine

We check functionality, responsive behavior, forms, key user journeys, and performance and accessibility basics.

06

Launch & hand over

We deploy or hand over the project with agreed access and documentation, and show you how everything works.

FAQ

Questions?
Answers.

Not sure which service is right for you? That's okay. Tell us what you're trying to achieve and we'll suggest a sensible starting point.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Startups, entrepreneurs, professionals, small and medium-sized businesses, and organizations that need websites or custom digital solutions. We review each inquiry to confirm the project is a good fit.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work with clients wherever they're based. Communication, scope, payment arrangements, and delivery milestones are agreed before work begins.

What technologies do you use?

Our core full-stack stack is MERN: MongoDB, Express.js, React, and Node.js. We may also use HTML, CSS, JavaScript or TypeScript, Git, REST APIs, and suitable hosting or third-party services depending on the project. A simple marketing website may not need a database or custom backend — we choose the architecture that fits.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, design requirements, functionality, integrations, content needs, and timeline. Share your requirements and we'll prepare a tailored estimate.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on scope, feedback, content readiness, and third-party integrations. We provide an estimated schedule after reviewing your requirements.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can review your current website and discuss improvements to its design, usability, content structure, performance, or functionality.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive behavior is part of our development approach. We test agreed pages and key layouts across common screen sizes and browsers.

Can you help with domain and hosting?

We can guide you through choosing a domain and hosting, and assist with setup or deployment where included in scope. Domain, hosting, and third-party subscription fees are normally paid separately by the client.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Maintenance can be arranged as a separate ongoing service. The plan specifies what's covered, response expectations, update frequency, backups, and any excluded work.

Can you integrate payment gateways?

We can integrate payment providers supported for your business location and customer market. Provider approval, merchant eligibility, transaction fees, and account setup are controlled by the payment provider.

Do you use AI to build projects?

We use AI-assisted tools where they improve research, planning, coding assistance, testing, and documentation. A human reviews project decisions and deliverables, and we remain responsible for the work we agree to provide.

Will I own my website after completion?

Ownership, source-code handover, licenses, third-party components, and access transfer are defined in the project agreement. We explain any exceptions before the project starts.

Can I request changes after the project is complete?

Small corrections within the agreed support period are handled per the project agreement. New features, redesigns, and changes outside the agreed scope may need a separate quote.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of your business and goals, the service you need, examples you like, required features, any existing website or brand assets, a budget range if available, and your preferred timeline.

Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No responsible provider can guarantee a specific Google ranking. We improve technical and on-page foundations, follow good practices, and monitor performance — but search results depend on many factors outside anyone's control.
